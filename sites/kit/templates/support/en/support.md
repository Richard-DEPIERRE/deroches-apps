# Help and contact

We will never ask for your password or your bank details.

## Restore your purchases

On a new phone, or after reinstalling {{name}}, open **Settings › Purchases › Restore purchases**. Use the same Apple
or Google account you bought with.

## Ask for a refund

Purchases in {{name}} are paid to Apple or Google: only they can refund them, under their rules.

- iPhone and iPad: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
- Android: [Google Play refunds](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637?hl=en)

To stop a subscription: on iPhone, open the iPhone’s Settings app, then **your name › Subscriptions**; on Android,
open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then **Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions**.

## Where your data is

What you save in {{name}} stays on your phone. We receive no copy of it, so we can’t see it or get it back for you.

- **Automatic backup**: this data is part of your iPhone’s iCloud backup and your Android phone’s Google backup, in
  your own account, when backup is on. It comes back when you set up a new phone from that backup.
- **By hand**: **Settings › Backup › Export a backup** saves a file to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or anywhere you
  like; **Restore a backup** replaces the data on the phone with that file’s.

What the app collects is set out in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy/).
