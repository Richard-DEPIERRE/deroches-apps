# Privacy policy

Last updated: {{updated}}. The publisher of the site {{domain}} and of the {{name}} app is named in the
[legal notice](/en/legal-notice/).

## On this site

- **No cookies.** The site sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device.
- **No forms, no accounts.** You don’t have to type anything to read the site.
- **Nothing from other sites.** The pages load no fonts, scripts or ads from anywhere else.
- **Visitor counts without personal data.** We count page views, how far pages are read and clicks to the App Store
  and Google Play, to learn which guides help. These totals are kept with Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine, with no
  IP address, no browser details (user agent) and no identifier of any kind: nobody can tell who read what, or follow
  you from one page or site to another.
- **Hosting.** Like any host, Cloudflare receives the technical details it needs to send you the pages, including your
  IP address. We don’t record them.

## If you write to us

If you write to {{support}}, we use your email address and your message to answer you, and for nothing else (our
legitimate interest in handling your request). Our email service receives them on our behalf. We keep them while we
handle your request and its follow-up. We never sell or share them.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict its use or receive a copy of it, and you can object to
its use (articles 15 to 21 of the GDPR). Write to {{support}}.

If you think your rights have not been respected, you can complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority,
online at [cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes) or by post: CNIL, Service des plaintes, 3 place de Fontenoy,
75007 Paris, France. You can also complain to the data protection authority of the country where you live.
