# Roulekm

Roulekm will be an iPhone and Android app that keeps your business mileage log. Log a trip in two taps: the date, where
from, where to, the purpose and the distance, typed in or worked out from two odometer readings. The app applies the
right rate to each vehicle, the IRS standard mileage rate in the US or HMRC’s approved mileage rates in the UK, and
keeps the year’s total. No account to create, no GPS running in the background, no trip limit: your trips stay on your
phone.
