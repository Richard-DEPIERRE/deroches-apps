Log a business trip in two taps and get your mileage at the right rate for each vehicle. No account, no GPS, no trip limit.

Roulekm is a private mileage log and calculator. It applies the IRS standard mileage rate (US) or HMRC’s approved mileage rates (UK), as published, and keeps your year’s total. For the UK, it counts the tax year from April 6 and moves cars and vans to the lower rate after the first 10,000 business miles.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
• No account: nothing to sign up for, no email asked.
• No GPS: no location permission, nothing running in the background, no drained battery, no trips recorded from your couch.
• No trip limit: unlimited trips and favorites, with or without Pro.
• Your trips stay on your phone.

FREE, WITH NO TIME LIMIT
• Unlimited trips: date, from, to, business purpose and distance, typed in or worked out from two odometer readings.
• Round trips and favorite trips, added in two taps from the home screen.
• One vehicle in use at a time, with its rate: cars, vans, pickups and panel trucks at the IRS rate; cars and vans, motorcycles and bicycles at HMRC’s. Changing cars? Archive the old one: its trips and total stay, and the new one is free.
• This year’s total on the home screen, with the rate in use and its year.
• Every year’s summary, current or past, per vehicle, with the trip list.
• Where the rates come from: each rate’s year, the page it is published on and the date it was read.
• A backup export in settings, to iCloud Drive or anywhere else.
• No ads.

ROULEKM PRO
• Your year’s mileage log as a PDF: date, places, business purpose and miles of every trip, the rate used and the totals per vehicle.
• CSV export, for a spreadsheet or your accountant.
• Recurring trips (“Mon–Fri, office → client site”): they wait on the home screen and count only once you confirm them, so days off stay out of your log.
• Several vehicles in use at once.

The same Pro in every plan: a one-time purchase you keep, or a yearly or weekly subscription. No trial that turns into a subscription. If Pro ends, nothing you entered or exported gets locked.

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period, at the price shown, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store account settings. The one-time purchase never renews.

Roulekm is a logging and calculation tool. It is not affiliated with any tax authority and does not give tax advice: checking what you can claim or deduct, and how you file, is your decision.
